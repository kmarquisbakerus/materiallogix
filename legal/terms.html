Where video output may be used

Every video the Studio produces carries a provenance record that says what produced it. Today that record says the same thing for every render: the file was made from your own footage with the editorial settings you chose, and no generative video model was used. This section governs what happens when that changes.

When a generative video engine is enabled, which one runs depends on where you are, and the provenance record names it. One engine we intend to offer — HunyuanVideo, the Pro Motion Engine — is licensed by its publisher for a defined territory that excludes the European Union, the United Kingdom and South Korea. That restriction travels with the output, not just with the software: the licence says its results may not be used, reproduced, modified, distributed or displayed outside that territory.

What that means in practice, from the day such an engine is enabled:

  • We do not render with that engine for customers in the excluded territories. Those regions use an engine with no territorial restriction, and the result is yours to use anywhere. The Studio decides this from your connection's region, which it asks of our edge network and not of your browser, and it does not offer a setting that overrides it.
  • If you are outside the excluded territories and you publish a file that engine produced — to a social platform, a website, or anywhere else that people in the excluded territories can reach it — you are responsible for that decision, as you are for every other use of your work.
  • If you intend to distribute worldwide and would rather not carry that restriction, choose the unrestricted engine before rendering. Whenever a restricted engine is offered, an unrestricted one is available to every customer on every plan beside it.
  • If your region cannot be confirmed, a render on a territorially licensed engine does not proceed until it can. Editorial renders of your own footage are unaffected.

We will tell you which engine produced a file, in the file's provenance record and in the Studio. If the engines available to you change, this section changes with them.
