Privacy

Effective August 2026

The short version: your creative work never touches our servers. The apps run on your machine, the engines run on your machine, and your files stay on your machine. We see almost nothing, on purpose.

What we never collect

  • Your images, video, audio, voice packs, scripts, or projects — processing is local; nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Analytics, tracking pixels, or behavioral profiles. There is no telemetry in the product.
  • Recordings of anyone. Voice packs you create stay on your device.

What we do collect, and why

  • License verification. Licensed installs periodically confirm the license is valid. That check sends your license key and a timestamp — nothing else. No files, no filenames, no usage data.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Stripe; we receive your email and plan so we can issue your key and receipts. We never see card numbers.
  • Email you send us. If you write to admin@materiallogix.com, we have that email.

Where data lives

Your projects live in your browser's local storage and on your disk, on your device. Clearing site data deletes them; we could not recover them for you, because we never had them.

Third parties

Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (hosting the website and the license check), Google Fonts (typefaces on this website). The apps themselves fetch an optional face-detection model from a public CDN when that feature is used; otherwise they make no third-party requests.

Your choices

Use the free tier without any account at all. Ask us to delete your purchase records at any time via admin@materiallogix.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes with it and licensed customers are notified by email.
