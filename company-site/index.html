MaterialLogix Studio / Active development

From source
to sign-off.

MaterialLogix Studio begins where capture or generation ends. It gives creators and teams one serious workflow to review Photo, Video, and Voice, make decisions, approve final work, and prepare it for delivery.

Visit MaterialLogix Studio ↗
  1. Source / generated
  2. Review
  3. Approve
  4. Package + deliver

Current builds are being validated for proof packaging, print-ready export, client review, and decision records. Camera RAW qualification and external delivery integrations remain in progress.

Local-first direction

Person-specific source media is being designed to stay on the customer’s computer by default. Optional remote compute will require an explicit customer choice and verified short-lived controls before release.
