Our point of view

01 / Company

Power should not require surrender.

Being locked out of a good tool is not only about price. Sometimes the learning curve is the barrier. Sometimes privacy is. Sometimes the product asks people to give up too much control just to use it.

Libra Side Technologies builds sophisticated products that remove friction without making the experience feel cheap, careless, or generic.

Founder

Problems first.
Products second.

Libra Side Technologies was founded in Washington, DC by Kevin Marquis Baker, a Black technology founder building from problems he encountered personally: creative software that was too difficult to use, connection platforms that did not feel safe enough, and digital systems that made everyday work harder than it needed to be.

The belief behind the company is simple: premium technology should feel sophisticated without feeling inaccessible.
