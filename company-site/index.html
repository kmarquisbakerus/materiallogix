Built from lived friction

Problems first.
Products second.

Libra Side Technologies was founded in Washington, DC by Kevin Marquis Baker, a Black technology founder building products from problems he encountered personally: creative software that was too difficult to use, connection platforms that did not feel safe enough, and digital systems that made everyday work harder than it needed to be.

The company is built around a simple belief: premium technology should feel sophisticated without feeling inaccessible.
