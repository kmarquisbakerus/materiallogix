Built in DC

Technology grounded in how people actually work and connect.

LibraSide began with problems founder Kevin Marquis Baker encountered directly: creative systems that obscured the path to a finished deliverable, and connection platforms that made boundaries feel secondary. The company turns that lived friction into products people can understand and control.

Kevin builds from lived friction first, then turns it into a product people can actually use. That through-line connects LibraSide’s work: less complexity, stronger boundaries, fairer access, and more control where it matters.

The visual language is intentional too—considered, current, and personal—while the company’s credibility remains anchored in product evidence.
