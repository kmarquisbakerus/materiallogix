The checks nobody else runs

Every approved asset passes a gauntlet before export is even allowed. These are measurements, not vibes — and the ones that get ads rejected are hard blocks.

01

Face under the caption

Face detection maps every face against each platform's real UI. A face more than half-buried under TikTok's caption block stops the export.

02

The upscale trap

A crop that would stretch past 1.6× is refused with the exact pixel numbers. No more soft heroes.

03

Rejected takes stay dead

Perceptual fingerprints of everything you've killed. A near-duplicate of a rejected asset cannot sneak back in round four.

04

Waxy skin

Skin regions carrying less detail than the rest of the frame — the classic AI tell — get flagged for the loupe before anyone else sees it.

05

Loudness compliance

Platforms duck or reject mis-leveled audio. We measure to the broadcast standard and normalize on render.

06

Sounds-AI voice tells

Digital-zero silence, flat dynamics, metronome pausing. A voice render isn't done until the tells your ear would catch have cleared.

07

Identity coverage

360° capture is tracked angle by angle. Missing 40° of someone's left side? You'll know before generation, not after.

08

Alt text & provenance

Approving without accessibility copy is a block. Content credentials are read from the file, never guessed.

Questions

Fair questions

What do I install?

Download MaterialLogix Studio for Windows, sign in to activate the device, and add only the engines you use. The browser demo shows the workflow; the installed application performs private local production.

Do my images, video, or voice ever upload to you?

Not in Local mode. Project media, renders, and voice packs remain on your device. Cloud mode is a separate, clearly labeled action: the app shows the execution location and maximum prepaid charge before you confirm the upload.

What about voice cloning — whose voices?

Only voices with consent: your own, or talent who signed a release. The capture flow is built around that rule, and we will not add scraping features. Your voice pack is yours — we never train on it, and it never leaves your machine unless you send a cloud job.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. Add the companion interface from the browser to your home screen, then use it for capture, review, approvals, and comments while the authorized Windows application handles local GPU work on the same network.

How big is the download?

The apps are about a megabyte. Engines install on demand, per feature — image upscaling is ~45 MB, video tools ~120 MB, the voice engine is the big one at a few GB and entirely optional. You only ever download the capability you use.

What happens when I hit a limit?

The job pauses and tells you exactly what it would cost to continue. Nothing auto-bills, nothing runs past a cap silently. Local rendering has no limits on paid tiers because it costs nothing to anyone.
