What you need to know

The short version.

Open MaterialLogix™ on the web or install it on Windows. Sign in once, choose a Studio, and use the access included with your plan.

Is the download a preview or the real product?

It is the actual Windows Studio. It begins with Free Preview access; a plan or export credit unlocks the matching paid capabilities without a second download.

Should I use the web or Windows version?

Use the web Studio when you want to begin quickly from a current browser. Install the Windows Studio for larger projects, direct access to files on that computer, and the strongest performance your computer supports.

Can I use my phone?

Yes. Enter the companion address shown in the Studio to control it from your phone and run guided capture scans.

What do the guided scans do?

They capture your face or body from every angle, so edits stay true to the real you.

When does work upload?

Never on its own. Files stay on your computer unless you pick a cloud job and approve its price.

What computer do I need?

Windows 10 or 11, at least 8 GB of memory, a current browser, and enough free project storage; faster hardware shortens larger jobs.

Where can I see my usage?

Usage shows each reservation, final charge, refund, promotional credit, and remaining wallet balance.
